How is the teaser mail sent?

A teaser mail is not sent by the campaign itself. When a campaign is scheduled, it only writes one job per recipient into the mailer queue, with the campaign id and the recipient's locale attached1. Nothing leaves the system at that point.

A separate worker drains that queue every thirty seconds. For each job it renders the teaser template in the recipient's language, checks that the recipient has not unsubscribed in the meantime, and hands the finished message to the transport2. A recipient who unsubscribed between scheduling and sending is skipped, and the job is marked as such rather than deleted.

The transport is the only place that talks to the outside world. It sends through the configured SMTP relay and records the provider's message id on the job, which is what the delivery report later matches against3. The relay's credentials come from the environment, not from the campaign.
