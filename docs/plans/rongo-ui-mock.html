Answer written

Teaser mail sending for abandoned carts runs fully automatically in shop-backend and consists of three parts: detecting the abandonment, a waiting period, and the sending itself.1

When a cart counts as abandoned

As soon as a cart has gone 30 minutes without a change and no order has been placed, it is marked as abandoned. Guest orders without a stored e-mail address are skipped.2

The waiting period

Four hours then pass before the first mail goes out. The delay is deliberate, so that customers who order shortly afterwards anyway do not get a pointless reminder.3

Sending and stop conditions

  1. An hourly job collects every cart that is due.
  2. At most two mails are sent per cart — the second after 48 hours.4
  3. If someone orders in the meantime, or opts out of marketing, sending stops immediately.5

Which part sits where

The mechanism is spread over three places. shop-backend orchestrates: it detects the abandonment, keeps the deadlines and decides whether anything is sent at all. The template and its text blocks live in mail-templates, which is pulled in as a library.6 The sending itself goes to vendor-mailer, a bought-in component.

One limitation applies to vendor-mailer: that component is not indexed in rongo. What is visible is how it is called and configured — what it does with the mail internally, such as delivery attempts or bounce handling, is not in the code readable here. That one question still has to be answered by someone on the team.
