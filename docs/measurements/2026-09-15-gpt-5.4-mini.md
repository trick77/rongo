# gpt-5.4-mini on both lanes: on par with MiMo, and the gate lane has to stay off

**Status: measured 2026-09-15 on the flow corpus (10 questions, twice). With
`BACKEND_LLM_MODEL=gpt-5.4-mini`, `BACKEND_LLM_GATE_MODEL=gpt-5.4-mini` and
the gate lane at `BACKEND_LLM_GATE_REASONING=off`, the rubric score is 26/30
in both runs, cited parts 18 and 19 of 30, nothing contradicted, nothing
forbidden asserted, no question asked back, no upstream failure. That is the
MiMo product's range (26-28/30, 19/30 cited, 2026-09-13-json-object.md).
`low` on the gate lane is not a setting: the 128-token gate calls spend the
whole cap on reasoning and return no text, and the routing judge fails.**

## Why

rongo-infra deploys behind a LiteLLM gateway that serves one chat model,
gpt-5.4-mini, and the call policy (#156) was ported from MiMo without a
measurement. The gateway is a pass-through with a cost header, so the arm
ran against api.openai.com directly: same weights, same wire.

## The arm

`hack/run-flow-eval.sh 'TestEvalMeasureAnswers$'` with
`BACKEND_EVAL_PRO_MODEL=gpt-5.4-mini BACKEND_EVAL_GATE_MODEL=gpt-5.4-mini`.
The judge stays on the product's deployment, MiMo Pro, so the grader is the
one that graded every earlier table. Corpus rebuilt from the pinned shas in
2026-09-10-flow-corpus.md (chunk counts reproduce: front-end 2146, user
348, orders 334).

What went out on the wire, from llmwire's log: temperature demoted on
every gate call (`temperature must be 1 on this model`, warned once), and
`reasoning_effort: none` for `off`. Both lanes through the one OpenAI key.

The four misses are the same two questions in both runs, and the same two
MiMo drops: the shipment when the queue is unavailable (1/2) and how the
invoice amount is computed (2/3). A model swap did not move them; they are
retrieval questions.

~15 s per question end to end, against MiMo Pro's minutes when it queues.
`BACKEND_LLM_TIMEOUT=5m` in the ConfigMap has room.

## The gate lane at `low`: broken, not worse

`BACKEND_EVAL_GATE_REASONING=low` (a new override in `answerLLM`, the
harness's name for `BACKEND_LLM_GATE_REASONING`) failed every question it reached (four, then stopped)
at `judge candidates`:

```
chat completion ended with finish_reason=length after 128 completion tokens
max_tokens=128 reasoning=low finish_reason=length content_chars=0 reasoning_tokens=128
```

`routeMaxTokens = 128` (`backend/internal/ask/route.go`) is the cap for a
one-line verdict; on this model an effort level bills its thinking against
the same cap and the verdict never arrives. The run was stopped. A gate
lane with reasoning on needs a cap raise on every parsed gate call first,
and there is nothing to gain: `off` already scores in MiMo's range.

## What it decides

- rongo-infra's ConfigMap stays at `GATE_TEMPERATURE=default`,
  `GATE_REASONING=off`, `REASONING=default`. Measured now, one arm, two runs.
- `off` is a hard requirement for gpt-5.4-mini on the gate lane as long as
  the parsed calls carry 128-token caps. A boot check for it would need the
  cap to be a policy the client knows about; not done.
- Not measured: `BACKEND_LLM_REASONING` at an effort level on the answer
  lane, and gpt-5.4 (non-mini) as the answer model. The key was temporary.
