# The gpt-5 series on the answer lane: 5.4 without thinking is the best arm, and the gate lane stays off

**Status: measured 2026-09-15 on the flow corpus (10 questions, twice per
arm, judge MiMo Pro throughout). gpt-5.4-mini on both lanes with the
ConfigMap policy scores 26/30 in both runs, MiMo's range. gpt-5.4 on the
answer lane at its default (no thinking) scores 28/30 in both runs, with the
most cited parts of any arm, and no effort level improves on it on any of
the three models. gpt-5.5 is not better than 5.4 and asserted the one
forbidden claim of the day. An effort level on the gate lane is not a
setting: the 128-token gate calls spend the whole cap on reasoning and the
routing judge fails.**

## Why

rongo-infra deploys behind a LiteLLM gateway that serves gpt-5.4-mini, and
the call policy (#156) was ported from MiMo without a measurement. The
gateway is a pass-through with a cost header, so every arm ran against
api.openai.com directly: same weights, same wire. With the key at hand,
the two larger siblings were measured on the answer lane as well, which
needed profiles for them in llmwire (trick77/llmwire#40, source-derived
from the model pages).

## The arms

`hack/run-flow-eval.sh 'TestEvalMeasureAnswers$'` with
`BACKEND_EVAL_PRO_MODEL` and `BACKEND_EVAL_GATE_MODEL` naming the lanes,
and two harness overrides added for this: `BACKEND_EVAL_GATE_REASONING`
and `BACKEND_EVAL_REASONING`, the harness's names for the product's
`BACKEND_LLM_GATE_REASONING` and `BACKEND_LLM_REASONING`. The judge stays
on the product's deployment, MiMo Pro, so the grader is the one that graded
every earlier table. Corpus rebuilt from the pinned shas in
2026-09-10-flow-corpus.md (chunk counts reproduce: front-end 2146, user
348, orders 334).

The gate lane is gpt-5.4-mini at `off` in every arm; only the answer lane
varies. On the wire, from llmwire's log: temperature demoted on every gate
call (`temperature must be 1 on this model`, warned once per model),
`reasoning_effort: none` for `off`, the effort word otherwise, nothing
when `default`. What `default` means differs per model: gpt-5.4-mini and
gpt-5.5 think when the field is omitted (medium), gpt-5.4's page names
`none` as its default and it does not.

No question was asked back, no upstream call failed, in any arm. The arms
ran two at a time, so the wall times are order of magnitude, not a
benchmark. Tokens are the harness's count of what went through the answer
pipeline, the same context in every arm; the differences are what the
model wrote and thought.

The misses are the same two questions in every arm, and the two MiMo also
drops: the shipment when the queue is unavailable (1/2) and how the invoice
amount is computed (2/3). Seven models and policies did not move them; they
are retrieval questions, not answer questions.

The forbidden claim: gpt-5.5 at medium, run 2, the guest-cart merge
question. The one assertion in 140 grades; the rubric's forbidden claims
are the confident-but-incomplete readings the corpus was built to expose.

## What it says

- **Thinking on the answer lane buys nothing here.** On mini, `low` and
  `medium` sit inside noise around `default` and cite fewer parts. On 5.4,
  `medium` equals `none` at twice the wall time. On 5.5, its default
  (medium) is below `off` on citations and produced the forbidden claim.
  The answer call has the whole context in front of it; there is nothing to
  work out.
- **gpt-5.4 without thinking is the best arm**: 28/28, cited 19 and 21, and
  reproducible where MiMo's product runs move between 26 and 28. At 2.50/15
  per 1M against mini's 0.75/4.50.
- **gpt-5.5 is not the step up.** 26-27 at 5.00/30, and the one forbidden
  claim. Not measured at `low` or `high`.
- **gpt-5.4-mini holds MiMo's range** at 26/26, for the deployment that has
  only mini.

## The gate lane at `low`: broken, not worse

`BACKEND_EVAL_GATE_REASONING=low` on gpt-5.4-mini failed every question it
reached (four, then stopped) at `judge candidates`:

```
chat completion ended with finish_reason=length after 128 completion tokens
max_tokens=128 reasoning=low finish_reason=length content_chars=0 reasoning_tokens=128
```

`routeMaxTokens = 128` (`backend/internal/ask/route.go`) is the cap for a
one-line verdict; on this series an effort level bills its thinking against
`max_completion_tokens` and the verdict never arrives. A gate lane with
reasoning on needs a cap raise on every parsed gate call first, and there is
nothing to gain: `off` already scores in MiMo's range.

## What it decides

- rongo-infra's ConfigMap stays at `GATE_TEMPERATURE=default`,
  `GATE_REASONING=off`, `REASONING=default` for gpt-5.4-mini. Measured.
- `off` is a hard requirement for the gpt-5 series on the gate lane as long
  as the parsed calls carry 128-token caps. A boot check for it would need
  the cap to be a policy the client knows about; not done.
- If the gateway ever serves gpt-5.4, the answer lane moves to it with
  `BACKEND_LLM_REASONING=default` (its default is none) and nothing else
  changes. gpt-5.5 is not worth its price on this corpus.
- Not measured: the gate lane on anything but gpt-5.4-mini, gpt-5.5 at
  `low` or `high`, and any of it through the actual gateway.
